Why we build this way

Most "security" apps ask for more permissions than the threats they claim to protect you from. They scan your phone, then ship everything they find to a server you don't control.

We do it differently. Every scan, every analysis, every risk score is computed entirely on your device. No accounts. No cloud. No analytics. No ads. No data leaves your phone — ever.

You can verify this yourself: PermissionScope uses exactly two permissions — local VPN (for DNS monitoring) and query installed apps (for scanning). That's it. No camera. No microphone. No contacts. No location.
